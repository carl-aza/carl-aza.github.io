Carl Aza

Arizona State University · General and Set-theoretic topology, ultrafilters, βℕ

I work on the Stone–Čech compactification βℕ, its depth filtration, and the finite semigroups that embed in it, together with the Ramsey-theoretic statements those embeddings yield. Related interests in combinatorics, mathematical logic, and symbolic dynamics.
